Frequently asked

Questions before the first meetup.

Is MeshUp a dating app?

No. MeshUp is activity-first. The primary goal is to help people find things to do and meet others through shared interests and real plans.

How does joining an activity work?

A member discovers an event, reviews the details, and sends a join request. The host can review the request before accepting or declining it.

What is the Reliability Score?

The Reliability Score is a proposed trust signal based on positive participation such as hosting and attending, balanced against late cancellations or no-shows. The scoring rules should remain transparent and include a fair dispute process.

How does MeshUp help people feel safer?

The product direction includes verification, visible community history, host approval, reporting, moderation, and event-level communication. People should still meet in appropriate public places and use personal judgment.

What are Memories?

Memories are photos and videos shared after activities. The feed celebrates completed events and shows that the local community is active.
